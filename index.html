❓ FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What is Immigration Interviews?

An AI-powered practice platform for the U.S. citizenship (naturalization) interview. Practice with AI officers with realistic voices, get an evaluation report after each mock interview, and study the official civics questions, reading, writing and vocabulary — fully bilingual English/Spanish.

How does the AI mock interview work?

Choose one of five AI officers, speak your answers out loud, and the officer responds with voice — asking the same types of questions as a real USCIS naturalization interview. At the end you get a personal evaluation: your score, your strengths, and the 3 most important things to improve.

Is it available in Spanish?

Yes — the platform works for immigrants from any country, and the whole interface is bilingual English/Spanish. The mock interview is in English, like the real one; instructions and feedback can be in Spanish.

Does it include the official civics questions?

Yes — both official versions: the 100-question (2008) test and the 128-question test, plus reading, writing and vocabulary practice.

How much does it cost?

The Free plan includes civics, reading, writing, vocabulary and one full mock interview. Paid plans are one-time payments with 30 days of access — no subscription, no automatic charges: Professional $19 founding price (regular $29) gives you 30 days of unlimited practice; renew anytime if you need more time before your interview. Premium $39 founding price (regular $49) adds live real-time interviews.

Is this affiliated with USCIS?

No. Immigration Interviews is an independent educational practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to USCIS or any government agency, and does not provide legal advice.
