Why these Illinois questions matter

Four questions on the official USCIS civics test depend on where you live: your U.S. Senators, your U.S. Representative, your Governor, and your state capital. If you live in Illinois, the Prairie State, these are the exact current answers USCIS officers expect.

You only need to name one of your two U.S. Senators — either Dick Durbin or Tammy Duckworth is a correct answer. Your U.S. Representative is different: it depends on your specific congressional district within Illinois, not the state as a whole, so look up your own representative using your home address before the interview.

Frequently asked questions

Who is one of Illinois's U.S. Senators now?

Illinois's current U.S. Senators are Dick Durbin and Tammy Duckworth. On the USCIS civics test you only need to correctly name one of them.

Who is the Governor of Illinois now?

JB Pritzker is the current Governor of Illinois.

What is the capital of Illinois?

The capital of Illinois is Springfield — this is different from the largest city in Illinois in most states, so don't mix them up.

Who is my U.S. Representative if I live in Illinois?

Your U.S. Representative depends on your specific congressional district within Illinois. Look up your representative using your home address at house.gov, then practice saying their name out loud before your interview.
